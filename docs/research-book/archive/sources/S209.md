> 模块接口初案：
> 
> ```text
> load_case(case_id) -> current_points, row_ids, pool[N,12,3], cameras, Q, R, W
> anchored_normals(case, pool) -> normals[N,12,2,3], fallback[N,12,2], diagnostics
> score_tensor(pool, normals, camera_group) -> costs[S,N,12,2], finite_mask
> choose(costs, common_views, offsets, aggregation) -> candidate_id[N], hypothesis_id[N]
> materialize(pool, candidate_id) -> output[N,3]
> evaluate_sealed_outputs(...) -> metrics  # 单独进程才读取GT
> ```
> 
> 推理使用的row_ids来自公开ROI几何裁剪索引而非laser ObsMask；评价mask留在评价器。
> 同位置不同名字的候选不增加质量权重；tie顺序固定KEEP优先、候选ID、方向ID。
