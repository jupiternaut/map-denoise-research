## Result and scope

One frozen primary method (`post_A_keep`) was evaluated on three new same-source DTU scenes (55/65/69), four ROIs each. Scene 40 is engineering adaptation, excluded from confirmation means. Primary fixed-native-row nearest-reference MSE improves by 36.08% / 33.79% for -3 / +3 mm ray perturbations, with all 12 ROIs improving at each sign. Native input worsens 8.12%, with no ROI improving. At -1 / +1 mm, MSE changes are respectively 2.00% worse / 9.74% better.

Zero and signed ray perturbations are sensitivity probes, not three spatial axes. They motivate investigating false movement near the incumbent and signed response asymmetry; they do not identify the causal mechanism or establish robustness to all noises. Three confirmation scenes are not 12 independent scenes or 420 independent experiments.

The controlled recovery experiment is complete. Remaining closeout evidence is external matched baselines, evaluation-support clarification and clean-environment end-to-end reproduction. No claim is made of general native denoising, thin-layer identity preservation, cross-source generalization, or superiority over mature methods. Default native output remains identity. New sensitivity studies would be a separate bounded experiment, not required to pretend this completed experiment is unfinished.
