Exact host: liekkas. Work only in this directory. All files in the old E0,
E0-D, V25 and formal-report directories are read-only. Do not publish, install
packages, use the GPU, or change an old checkpoint. Disable Python bytecode.

with apply_patch. Programs may create their own outputs here. A result directory
must not be overwritten. Evaluation truth and generator mechanism labels are not
inputs to the operator; declared calibration labels are allowed in calibration.

Keep negative results. Same-family new seeds are not real-world validation.
