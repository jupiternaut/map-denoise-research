# Chart contract (before rendering)

Question: after exact finite search removes optimization error, how do observations,
representation and feedback budget affect the remaining prediction/action error?
Takeaway must follow measured results; no preselected claim that adaptive is better.

Surface: in-app chart if the available Data Analytics chart widget can render the
reviewed summary; numeric raw CSV/JSON remain the durable scientific artifacts.
One grouped comparison chart with 3 families x 3 methods at m=32, uniform training,
full-domain prediction error. There are 9 populated aggregates expected. Use exact
values plus underlying unique-target counts and sample repetitions in its table.
Optional second chart: feedback error by budgets0..4 and strategy for multiplexer
goals only, discrete ordered resource axis, not a chronological trend.

Palette: blue for exact/adaptive, orange for prefix/batch, grey for affine/fixed.
Non-color encoding: method legend and distinct groups; error is a proportion or
percentage with zero anchored and explicit units. No confidence bars based on
correlated rows. Neutral descriptive titles. Source is the exact named local result
file, experiment seed and finite-suite scope. Final QA inspects rendered values,
labels and denominator. If widget unavailable/failed, retain a simple table rather
than add a new charting dependency.
